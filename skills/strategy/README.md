# strategy

Develops, pressure-tests, or refines a strategy with the user over several turns: define the desired state, diagnose the current one, work backwards to find pathways, work forwards to stress-test them, then choose and make the choice usable. Any action can be refined into a smaller strategy and handed off.

## Why it exists

Agents produce bad strategy by default: goals restated as strategy, generic SWOT inventories, and long task lists with no real choice. This skill forces a diagnosis, a guiding policy that says what does not get effort, and signals that say when to change course.

It is a practical synthesis, not a validated framework:

- [Richard Rumelt, The perils of bad strategy](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-perils-of-bad-strategy): diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent action, and the marks of bad strategy. Consult *Good Strategy Bad Strategy* itself before making book-specific claims.
- [Deltares, Dynamic Adaptive Policy Pathways](https://www.deltares.nl/en/expertise/areas-of-expertise/sea-level-rise/dynamic-adaptive-policy-pathways): alternative pathways, conditions under which an action stops working, and monitoring signals. Borrowed without its modelling and governance overhead, which suits decades-long infrastructure, not startups.
- Backcasting and rolling-wave planning are standard terms for the backwards reasoning and progressive refinement. The state-based model, the lightweight representation, and the delegation boundary are design choices for this skill.

## Layout

- `SKILL.md`: the workflow.
- `references/examples.md`: worked team and personal strategies, including a written hand-off.
- `references/refine-and-delegate.md`: refining a transition and what a hand-off contains.
- `references/output-and-maintenance.md`: the default output skeleton, pathway table, and updating an existing strategy.

## Iterating

- Test at each depth: a quick personal question should get a few paragraphs in one turn, and a company strategy should stop at the desired-state and diagnosis checkpoint for correction.
- Watch for over-collaboration (a questionnaire up front, approval after every stage) as much as for a finished strategy delivered without the user's input.
