# Demo principles and sources

Reviewed 21 October 2026. These are practitioner recommendations, adapted for internal demos rather than investor pitches.

- [Y Combinator: A Guide to Demo Day Presentations](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/), Geoff Ralston, 25 July 2016. Develop a clear story before building slides; keep each visual to one point; use relevant pictures and very little text; improve through practice, critique, and selective removal. Apply those presentation principles here, rather than the article's fundraising aims or preference against live demos in investor pitches.
- [Duarte: Presentation storytelling 101: Your audience is the hero](https://www.duarte.com/blog/presentation-storytelling-audience-is-hero/), Phoebe Perelman, 15 July 2024. Centre the audience's challenges and useful change. Adaptation: select proof points by audience value rather than how impressive the presenter's work sounds.
- [Atlassian: Sprint demo](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/project-management/sprint-demo/). Demonstrate working outcomes, tailor to stakeholders, and rehearse. Adaptation: show a realistic path and time the actions as well as the narration.

The hook → problem → brief effort → demonstration sequence comes from the skill request. The one-to-three proof-point default and 75% time target are practical editorial choices, not experimentally established thresholds. Change them when the format warrants it while keeping the demonstration focused and comfortably within its budget.
