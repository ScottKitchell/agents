# create-demo

Plans and builds internal demos: one audience takeaway, one to three proof points, a hook → problem → effort → demonstration story, and the slides, recording, document, or post to deliver it.

## Why it exists

Agent-drafted demos tour every feature, narrate the development history, and overrun the slot. This skill makes the agent pick what earns attention, build the story around visuals, and plan for less time than it has.

Sources, reviewed October 2026 and adapted for internal demos rather than investor pitches:

- [Y Combinator, A Guide to Demo Day Presentations](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/): story before slides, one point per visual, very little text, improve by practice and cutting. Its fundraising aims and wariness of live demos don't apply here.
- [Duarte, Your audience is the hero](https://www.duarte.com/blog/presentation-storytelling-audience-is-hero/): choose proof points by value to the audience, not by how impressive the work sounds.
- [Atlassian, Sprint demo](https://www.atlassian.com/agile/project-management/sprint-demo/): show working outcomes on a realistic path, tailored to stakeholders, and rehearse.

The story sequence came from my own brief. The one-to-three proof points and the 75% time target are editorial heuristics, not research findings; change them when a format warrants it.

## Iterating

- Test with a short slot (five minutes) and a long one, live and recorded. Check the plan fits comfortably and spends most of its time on the demonstration.
- File-format mechanics (slides, documents) belong to other skills. Keep this one about the demo.
